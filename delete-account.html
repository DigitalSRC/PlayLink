Delete Your PlayLink Account

PlayLink by Silvenari

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PlayLink and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Settings, tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete My Account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't sign in, email support@silvenari.com from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my PlayLink account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email address, and password
  • Your profile: username, display name, city, games, brackets, and preferences
  • Your points, score, record, Shop purchases, rewards, and rivals
  • Your seats in groups. Groups you hosted pass to another player, and a group left with no one in it is removed.

What is kept

Results of rounds you played stay as part of the other players' history, so their points are unchanged, but they no longer link to anything about you. Our host's backups are overwritten within 7 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
