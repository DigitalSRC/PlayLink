Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · PlayLink by Silvenari

The short version: PlayLink keeps only what it needs to find you a table and keep score. It shows no ads, sells nothing about you, and has no analytics or tracking inside the app. You can delete your account and everything in it from the Profile tab at any time.

Who we are

PlayLink is an app for finding Magic: The Gathering Commander games near you. It is made by Silvenari, an independent developer. Questions about this policy or your data go to support@silvenari.com.

What the app collects

WhatWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
Username and display nameSo other players can recognize you.
The city or area you type inTo show games and store events near you. The app never reads your phone's GPS.
Play preferences: games, formats, brackets, and what you won't play againstTo match you with suitable groups and rivals.
Groups you post or join, and round resultsTo run groups and to award points, wins, losses, and draws.
Points, score, purchases of cosmetic items, rivals, and starter rewardsTo run the points system, the Shop, the leaderboard, and rival matching.
When you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted the TermsAs a record of that confirmation.

On your phone, the app stores your sign-in session in the device's secure storage, and a few settings (light or dark theme, your picked Rival, whether you have seen the guide) in the app's own storage. Removing the app removes them.

Who can see it

Other signed-in PlayLink players can see your profile: your username, display name, title and cosmetics, points and record, games and brackets, the city you entered, and what you won't play against. Players in a group with you see the group's roster and results. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Services we use

We share data only with the services that run PlayLink, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in (servers in the United States).
  • Resend delivers password-reset emails, so it receives your email address when you ask for one.
  • Open-Meteo turns the city you type into map coordinates. Only the place name is sent, never who typed it.
  • Wizards of the Coast's store locator is where store event listings come from. Only an area's coordinates are sent, never anything about you.
  • Expo builds and delivers the app. Apple and Google distribute it under their own privacy policies.

This website, though not the app, uses Google Analytics to count visits, and stores the details you give if you join the early-access list.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed straight away: your login, profile, preferences, purchases, rewards, and seats in groups. Groups you hosted pass to another player. Past round results stay so other players' history and points are unchanged, but they no longer link to anything about you. Copies in our host's backups are overwritten within 7 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See or change your data: everything you entered is on your Profile tab and can be edited there.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Ask us anything: email support@silvenari.com for a copy of your data or any other request. We answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, or the UK), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Adults only

PlayLink is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database enforces who may read and change what, so the app itself cannot be used to change another player's data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
